Shipping Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Thank you for shopping with LéaRivoli. This policy explains how orders are processed and delivered.

Order Processing

Some products are made to order after your order is placed.

Order processing and production time: 1-3 days

Your order confirmation will be sent by email after you place an order. Dispatch and tracking notifications will be sent when available and applicable to the delivery method.

Delivery Methods and Estimated Delivery Times

Our current estimated delivery time is 3–12 days.

This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. Actual delivery times may vary depending on the product, production time, destination, carrier and other circumstances.

Important: 3-12 days do not include the order processing time

Available delivery options and applicable charges will be displayed at checkout before you complete your purchase.

If no delivery period has been agreed, applicable EU consumer rules generally require delivery without undue delay and within 30 days, unless an exception applies.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are displayed at checkout before you place your order.

Shipping rates: Shipping rates is depending on which country the package is shipped to

Delivery Destinations

We currently accept orders for delivery to eligible destinations within the European Union only.

The countries available for delivery will be shown at checkout. If your country is not available, we are unable to accept delivery to that destination at this time.

Tracking

Where tracking is available, we will send tracking details by email when they become available. Tracking information may take some time to update after dispatch.

Tracking availability: available for all orders

Multiple Shipments

If products in an order are fulfilled by different suppliers or facilities, they may arrive in separate packages and at different times.

Delivery Address

Please check that your delivery address is correct before placing your order. If you notice an error, contact learivoliofficial@gmail.com as soon as possible.

We cannot guarantee that an address can be changed after an order has entered production or been dispatched.

Lost, Damaged or Delayed Packages

If your order is lost, damaged, incomplete or significantly delayed, contact learivoliofficial@gmail.com and include your order number.

We will investigate the issue with the relevant fulfilment provider or carrier and work with you to resolve it.

Using a third-party supplier or carrier does not remove our obligations to you under applicable consumer law. Where the law provides that the seller remains responsible for the goods until you or your designated recipient takes physical possession of them, we will respect that rule.

Customs, Taxes and Import Charges

This store currently accepts orders for delivery within the European Union only.

The exact tax treatment may depend on the product and the destination.

We will update this section if our delivery destinations or fulfilment arrangements change.

Order Changes and Cancellations

If you need to change or cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible. We may be unable to change an order once it has entered production or been dispatched.

This does not limit any statutory right of withdrawal or other consumer right that applies to your purchase.
