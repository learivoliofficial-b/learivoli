Return and Refund Policy

At LéaRivoli, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. This policy explains how returns, cancellations and refunds work for orders placed through our online store.

Returns and Cancellations

If you are a consumer in the European Union, you generally have the right to withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days of receiving your goods, without giving a reason, unless a legal exception applies.

If your order is delivered in multiple shipments, the withdrawal period generally starts when you receive the last item.

To exercise your right of withdrawal, contact us at learivoliofficial@gmail.com within the 14-day period. After notifying us, you must send the goods back within 14 days.

Items should be handled only as much as necessary to inspect their nature, characteristics and functioning. You may be responsible for any diminished value caused by handling beyond what would reasonably be permitted in a physical store.

Return shipping costs: You are responsible for the direct cost of returning an item when exercising your right of withdrawal, provided this has been communicated to you before purchase and applicable law permits it.

We do not provide free return shipping or a prepaid return label for ordinary change-of-mind returns unless we expressly agree otherwise.

Please contact us before sending anything back so we can confirm the correct return address and instructions.

Starting a Return or Cancellation

To request a return, cancellation or refund, email learivoliofficial@gmail.com and include your order number and the reason for your request, if you wish to provide one.

You do not have to give a reason to exercise your statutory right of withdrawal.

Please do not send products to an address supplied by a fulfilment provider unless we have confirmed that it is the correct return address.

You may request cancellation before an order enters production or is dispatched. Once an order has entered production, we cannot guarantee that a cancellation request can be accommodated at that stage. This does not affect any statutory withdrawal rights that apply.

Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

Please inspect your order when it arrives and contact us at learivoliofficial@gmail.com if an item is damaged, defective, incorrect or does not conform to its description.

Include your order number and a description of the issue. Photographs may help us assess the problem.

Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected. Where applicable law requires a repair, replacement, price reduction, refund or reimbursement of return costs, we will comply with those requirements.

Exceptions to the Right of Withdrawal

Certain products may be excluded from the statutory right of withdrawal where a specific legal exception applies, for example goods made to the consumer’s specifications or clearly personalised for that consumer.

A product is not automatically excluded from the right of withdrawal simply because it is produced or printed after an order is placed.

Any applicable exception will be explained before purchase. Statutory rights concerning defective, damaged, misdescribed or non-conforming goods remain unaffected.

Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item for a different size or product, contact learivoliofficial@gmail.com before returning it. Exchanges are subject to availability and our fulfilment arrangements.

Your statutory rights, including your right of withdrawal where applicable, are not affected by this section.

Refunds

If you validly withdraw from a purchase, we will refund the payments we received from you, including the cost of the least expensive standard delivery option offered, where required by law. Additional costs for a more expensive delivery option do not have to be refunded where the law permits this.

We will issue the refund without undue delay and no later than 14 days after we are informed of your decision to withdraw. We may withhold the refund until we receive the goods or you provide evidence that you have sent them back, whichever occurs first, where permitted by law.

Refunds will normally be made to the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise. We will not charge a fee for processing the refund.

Your statutory rights under applicable consumer-protection law remain unaffected, including rights relating to faulty goods and delivery failures.
